Privacy Policy

GraphicDesignNY · Last updated September 24, 2026

GraphicDesignNY (“we,” “us”) operates a self-hosted social media scheduling tool used to publish our own content to our own connected social media accounts.

Information we access

Through the official APIs of connected platforms (for example, Pinterest), we access only the accounts we ourselves own and authorize — in order to create, schedule, and publish posts, and to read the boards and pins on those accounts for that purpose. We do not access other users’ data.

How we use it

We use this access solely to schedule and publish our own content. We do not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Storage

Access tokens and content are stored on our own self-hosted, access-restricted server and are used only to operate the scheduling tool.

Data retention & removal

We retain authorization only for as long as an account remains connected. Disconnecting an account, or revoking access in the platform’s own settings, removes our access.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the revised version at this same address.

For any privacy question, contact us at graphicdesignny@gmail.com.
